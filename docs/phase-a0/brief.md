
# The BRIMM: rules for this project

Added by brimm-video. These rules come before everything above them in this file.

- **A draft only.** This video is a draft for The BRIMM. Never post or publish anything.
- **Commands never run:** `hyperframes publish`, `cloud`, `lambda`, `cloudrun`, `auth` or `feedback`.
- **Renders on this Mac only.** Renders and exports are made on this Mac, never in the cloud.
- **Sharing.** If asked to share, say that Sallie shares from the app's Share button, and only with "Only people you invite". Never set a link to "Anyone with the link".
- **No generated people.** No HeyGen avatars, HeyGen voices or HeyGen Video 1 clips without Jason's yes. No generated people who could pass as real, and never a generated Sallie, face or voice.
- **The brand** is the BRIMM brand kit.
  - **Colours:** navy #1F2D42, fog #5C6D7E, light gray #9EA9B2, dew #CED3D6, sand #C1AD97, dune #CEBEAC, mist #E8E3DA, smoke #F8F7F5.
  - **Type:** Josefin Sans. Light for sentences, Regular for a line, Medium capitals with letter spacing 0.10 for names and headlines.
  - **Name on screen:** Sallie Ogden, unless the request names someone else.
- **Captions.**
  - A wide main video has no captions drawn on it.
  - Reels have captions.
  - On an upright Reel, keep words out of the top 14% and the bottom 35% of the frame.
- **The transcript.** The words and their times are in `transcript.json`, made on this Mac by brimm-video. Use it, and do not transcribe again.
- **Versions.** When asked what has changed, or to go back, use `npx hyperframes history` (list, show, restore, peek). Describe each change in plain words, with its time in the video.
- **Cutting.** Cut whole phrases at pauses, never in the middle of a word.
