## The brand guide

**Colours.**

| Name | Value | | Name | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#1F2D42` | | Sand | `#C1AD97` |
| Fog | `#5C6D7E` | | Dune | `#CEBEAC` |
| Light gray | `#9EA9B2` | | Mist | `#E8E3DA` |
| Dew | `#CED3D6` | | Smoke | `#F8F7F5` |

A secondary palette is for accents, mainly in photographs. The guide says never to use it as a background colour or for text: Blush `#CCA497`, Rose `#EDD6D3`, Forest `#4E563D`, Moss `#727A64`, Sage `#979B8E`.

**Typefaces.** Sweet Sans Pro in three weights. The type page shows a headline in Medium capitals with wide spacing, a line in Regular and a paragraph in Light, each with its weight's name under it. It does not say in words what each weight is for. It shows two words in Sinthya, a handwritten script, and gives no rule for it. Two alternates are allowed only where Sweet Sans cannot be used, and the guide names Kartra: Lucida Grande for emails, Varela for sales pages.

**Logos.** Six marks: the wordmark THE BRIMM (primary, horizontal), a stacked wordmark, a stamp icon, a monogram icon, and two that add "with Sallie Holder" in the script.

**Shapes and lines.** A circle, an arch, a rounded square, a rounded rectangle and an oval, used as photo frames, as blocks that hold text, or as thin outlines. Thin lines in the background, three at most in one graphic, in a colour close to the background.

**Design tips.** Four of them. Mist, smoke or dew as the background, for a light and airy feel. Subtle textured backgrounds, to add interest without clutter. Photographs with neutral backgrounds, and clothing close to the brand colours. Light in colour, minimal, with white space.

## What changed in `presets.md`

- **The brand kit** is the guide's: its eight colours by their own names, Sweet Sans Pro in its three weights, a headline role (Medium capitals, letter spacing 0.10) and the script Sinthya. The secondary palette is recorded only so that it is refused for words and plates.
- **Plain** kept its white words and soft shadow. Only its typeface changed.
- **Boxed** is navy words on a mist box with rounded corners (10.9 to 1).
- **Bold** kept its capitals. Its accent is navy on a sand box (6.4 to 1).
- **Magazine** became **Signature**: the guide's own headline in spaced capitals on a navy block, with a word or two in the script. The guide has no serif.
- **A fourth look, Bright,** is the nearest a grade comes to light and airy. Natural is still the default. Moody stays because Jason named it, and the report says it goes against the guide.
- **Every count of characters** was worked again, because Sweet Sans Pro is wider than the stand-in the first figures were measured in. Plain holds 18 characters a line, not 20.
- **The logo** has files, so the report names a mark for the corner: the stacked wordmark, white over dark footage and navy over light. It shows on the three upright clip jobs. Nothing is drawn on the 16:9 episode, so there it does not show.
- **After two more checks** of that version, five things about the brand were corrected. The kit lists two stand-ins for Sweet Sans Pro, in order: Varela, then Lucida Grande, which is on every Mac. The script's smallest size is 2.5% of the frame's height on an upright frame and 3.3% on a wide one. In a Final Cut file Bold's stressed word stays white until a title with a sand box is made in Motion, because sand is never a colour for words over the picture. The characters that fit a line are worked out for the face that is drawn, not stored. And MyFonts prices its licence by the user.

## The typefaces, in short

`presets.md`, section 1.1, has the detail and the sources.

| Typeface | Who sells it | Does a licence cover words in a video | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sweet Sans Pro | MVB Fonts, through Adobe Fonts, MyFonts and Type Network | Yes at all three, by their own pages | Adobe Fonts: nothing beyond a Creative Cloud subscription. MyFonts: from $79.00 a style, priced by the number of users. Type Network: not confirmed |

| Varela, the guide's alternate | Free, on Google Fonts, under the SIL Open Font License | Yes | None |
