# Sallie Ogden's style (The BRIMM)

Calm, premium and warm: light grounds, navy words, soft shapes, thin lines and a lot of room. Videos are for women
founders with established businesses who want more sales, better profit and an easier-to-run business. Direct,
unhurried, real numbers, one clear next move. Never a generic tech startup: no gradients, neon, glass, emoji, hype
or hustle language. The name on screen is Sallie Ogden; the company is The BRIMM.

## References
- None sent. Pace, captions and motion come from the BRIMM design system (DESIGN.md, Nov 2026) and the "calm, editorial" row.

## Type
- Display: Josefin Sans Medium 500, upright, capitals with wide tracking (0.10em): hooks, thesis words, big figures
- Text: Josefin Sans Regular 400 (Light 300 for long lines): captions, labels, text boxes
- Mono: none. Josefin Sans Medium carries numbers, units, kickers (Label style: capitals, 0.20em)
- Allison (script): one word or two beside a headline, once per video, on a plain ground only, never over footage, never below 40px. Not a kit font, so add it as a hand-built element when wanted.

## Colour
- Accent Sand #C1AD97 on dark and footage; #8A7358 on the light ground (Sand fails there). Accent marks emphasis.
- Negative and positive have no brand colours. Losses use a lighter navy-hue blue #7FA0C4 (#3F5F86 on light); gains use white (Navy on light). Always pair with an icon or a word ("down", "up"); colour never carries meaning alone.
- Grounds: light Smoke #F8F7F5, dark Navy #1F2D42. Flat (no grid), 16px corners. Lines 1px, at most three, each a shade from its ground.
- Secondary palette (blush, rose, forest, moss, sage) lives only in photographs.

## Captions
Navy box with Smoke words, Sand for the emphasis word, sentence case, medium size. No all caps in captions (caps are for headlines).

## Motion
Calm. Things ease in with a fade and an 8px rise, settle and stop. No bounce, spin, shake or loop.

## Sound
Light: a few soft effects. No music unless a track is handed over.

## Hi-fi
Two-line hook: a plain line over a big capitals display line. Captions in short phrases. Cards carry a beat now and then, light and dark alternating, flat with thin sand lines. Small settle on cuts. Type behind the head sometimes. Colour, no black and white. On upright video keep words and logos out of the top 14% and bottom 35%.

## Lo-fi
Phrase captions, three words at a time, in a Navy box. Native-looking text boxes for hook and CTA. Overlays and backdrops now and then. No hard zooms.

## Showcase
One big word stamped letter by letter behind the head. Transitions: push and reveal only. Medium density; choose a motif per video.

## Never
- Whip, spin, bounce, glow, gradient, neon, emoji, hustle language
- "Sallie Holder"; retired lockups; recoloured, stretched or shadowed logos; more than one logo per view
- White, mist or smoke words on sand, dune, light gray or dew; sand or gray words over a photo
- Allison over a photo, in capitals, or as a sentence
