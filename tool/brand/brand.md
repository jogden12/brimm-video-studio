# The BRIMM's brand

For every video and graphic made in this project. It is The BRIMM's brand guide, with Josefin Sans in place of the guide's Sweet Sans Pro and Allison in place of its script, Sinthya. Where the two differ, this file wins.

## The brand

- **The BRIMM** is Sallie Ogden's business strategy for women founders.
- **The name on screen is Sallie Ogden.** Never "Sallie Holder", and never a logo that says it.
- **The company's name is The BRIMM,** with BRIMM in capitals, in titles, captions and every word on screen. Whatever a transcript says, "Brim" or "brim" is BRIMM.
- **The look:** light, airy and minimal, with white space. Backgrounds are mostly mist, smoke or dew. Navy carries the words.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#1F2D42` | The brand's colour: words on light backgrounds, headlines, logos on light backgrounds, a solid title card |
| Sand | `#C1AD97` | Boxes, shapes and lines. As words, only on navy |
| Dune | `#CEBEAC` | Boxes, shapes and lines. As words, only on navy |
| Mist | `#E8E3DA` | The usual background, and the box behind words over the picture |
| Fog | `#5C6D7E` | Lines, outlines and quiet details. As words, only on smoke or white |
| Light gray | `#9EA9B2` | Lines on dew, and outlines |
| Dew | `#CED3D6` | A background |
| Smoke | `#F8F7F5` | A background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Words and logos on navy or over the picture. Not a background |

The guide's secondary palette (blush, rose, forest, moss and sage) belongs to photographs: clothes, props, flowers. Never use it for words, backgrounds, shapes or lines.

### Words on colour

Small words need a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 (the WCAG formula).

- **Yes:** navy on smoke (13.0), mist (10.9), dew (9.2), dune (7.7), sand (6.4) or light gray (5.8). White (13.9), mist (10.9), dune (7.7) or sand (6.4) on navy. Fog on smoke (5.0) or white (5.3), for secondary words.
- **No:** fog on mist (4.2) or dew (3.5); navy on fog (2.6); white, mist or smoke on sand, dune, light gray or dew (1.1 to 2.4).
- **Over the picture:** white words with a soft shadow, or navy words on a mist box with rounded corners. Never sand, dune, fog or light gray words over the picture.

## Typefaces: Josefin Sans and Allison

Both are from Google Fonts, under the SIL Open Font License, and in this project's `assets/fonts/`. `index.html` already declares them.

| Face | CSS | File |
|---|---|---|
| Josefin Sans Light | `font-family: "Josefin Sans"; font-weight: 300` | `josefin-sans-latin-300-normal.woff2` |
| Josefin Sans Regular | `font-family: "Josefin Sans"; font-weight: 400` | `josefin-sans-latin-400-normal.woff2` |
| Josefin Sans Regular Italic | `font-family: "Josefin Sans"; font-weight: 400; font-style: italic` | `josefin-sans-latin-400-italic.woff2` |
| Josefin Sans Medium | `font-family: "Josefin Sans"; font-weight: 500` | `josefin-sans-latin-500-normal.woff2` |
| Allison | `font-family: "Allison"; font-weight: 400` | `Allison-Regular.ttf` |

- **Josefin Sans carries the words.** Allison is a script accent, used sparingly. No other typeface, and none from the network.
- **One italic, and nothing bolder than Medium.** Regular Italic is the only slanted face: never slant another weight, and never make words heavier than Medium.

### Text styles

| Style | Face | Case | Letter spacing | For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | Josefin Sans Medium | Capitals | 0.10 em | Titles, title cards, a name in a lower third |
| Label | Josefin Sans Medium | Small capitals | 0.20 em | A step number, a word above a headline, a tag |
| Line | Josefin Sans Regular | As written | None | A single line: a lower third's second line, a short statement |
| Subtitle | Josefin Sans Regular Italic | As written | None | A subtitle under a headline, a quotation, a second voice |
| Sentence | Josefin Sans Light | As written | None | Sentences and short paragraphs, with a line height of 1.35 |
| Caption | Josefin Sans Medium | As spoken | None | Captions on Reels |
| Accent | Allison | Lower case or title case | None | A word or two beside a headline, at least as large as the headline |

A lower third: SALLIE OGDEN in the headline style, with a line under it in Regular. Allison goes on a plain background (mist, smoke, dew or navy), never straight over the picture, and at most once in a frame.

## Logos

The logos chosen for this project are in `assets/logos/`, where it has them; their names say which mark and which colour. The marks are the wordmark (THE BRIMM on one line, the primary logo), the stacked wordmark (THE above BRIMM), the monogram (a B inside a ring of THE BRIMM) and the stamp (a ring of THE BRIMM around plain circles).

- Navy on light backgrounds; white on navy or over the picture.
- The wordmark for wide frames and end cards; the stacked logo for upright frames; the monogram as a small mark in a corner; the stamp as a seal or a quiet decoration.
- One logo in a frame, with clear space around it. Never stretch, squash, rotate, crop, recolour or outline a logo, and never give it a shadow or a glow.
- Never a logo that says "with Sallie Holder", and never a logo from outside this project.

## Shapes, lines and backgrounds

- **Shapes:** a circle, an arch, a rounded square, a rounded rectangle and an oval: photo frames, blocks that hold words, or thin outlines.
- **A box that holds words:** corners rounded to 15% of its height, and padding of about one and a half capital heights.
- **Lines:** thin, and at most three in a graphic, each a shade darker than its background: light gray on dew, sand on mist, mist on smoke.
- **Backgrounds:** mist, smoke or dew, and navy for a bold title card. A subtle texture is fine; a busy pattern is not.
- **End cards:** An end card starts after the last words are said, never over them, and it never cuts the recording: the video runs to its end, and the card follows it.
- **Upright Reels:** words and logos stay out of the top 14% and the bottom 35% of the frame.
