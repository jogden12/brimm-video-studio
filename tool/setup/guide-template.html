Getting Started

This guide is for {{machine}}. It is version {{version}} ({{build}}) of BRIMM Video Studio, set up on {{date}}.

Your drop folder

Videos go into your drop folder, which is in Finder under: {{drop}}.

To keep it close at hand, add it to Finder's sidebar:

  1. In Finder, open the drop folder.
  2. Drag its folder icon from the title bar of the window into the sidebar, on the left.

Finished videos you export from HyperFrames go to: {{exports}}.

Dropbox

BRIMM Video lives in Dropbox, in Finder under: {{root}}. Keep it on this Mac so it opens without a connection:

  1. In Finder, find the BRIMM Video folder in Dropbox.
  2. Hold Control and click the folder, or right-click it.
  3. Choose Make available offline.

When you drag a video from Dropbox into your drop folder, hold Option, so Dropbox keeps its copy.

Never export a video into the drop folder: BRIMM Video Studio would take it in as a new video.

HyperFrames settings

  • In HyperFrames, the agent is Claude Code with Auto access, never Full.
  • Telemetry stays off.
  • HyperFrames asks you to sign in to HeyGen the first time it opens.
  • Never render in the cloud: every video is made on this Mac.
  • The BRIMM does not use an AI avatar, lip-sync, or an AI-generated Sallie, her face or her voice, for now.

Starting in the Claude app

  1. Open the Claude app and choose its Code tab.
  2. Choose the folder BRIMM Video Studio, which is in your home folder ({{studio}}).
  3. Tell Claude what you would like to do with a video.
